Your guide

I’m Johan. I’ve been guiding on Table Mountain since 2010, and I still find new lines through its gorges, buttresses and ridges.

Whether it’s your first hike up the mountain or you want to climb its sandstone faces, I’ll choose the route that suits your fitness, your experience and the day’s weather.

I guide in English and Afrikaans.

What we can do on the mountain

Four ways to experience Table Mountain. Mix them in one day if you like.

  • Hiking

    From gentle contour paths to steep gorges up to the summit plateau. Routes for every fitness level.

    All levels

  • Trail running

    Run the mountain’s trail network with someone who knows every junction. Ideal if you want to cover a lot of ground.

    Fit runners

  • Scrambling

    Hands-on routes over rock, ledges and chains, somewhere between a hike and a climb. Bigger views, more adrenaline.

    A head for heights

  • Rock climbing

    Roped climbing on Table Mountain sandstone, for beginners trying it for the first time and experienced climbers looking for classic lines.

    Beginner to advanced

Safety first, every time

Table Mountain’s weather can turn in minutes. I watch the forecast, carry first-aid and emergency gear, and change the plan when the mountain says so.

  • Small groups
  • Routes matched to your level
  • First-aid and emergency gear on every trip
  • Planning based on the weather
